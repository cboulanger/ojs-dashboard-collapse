The left menu of OJS's editorial backend takes about a third of a laptop screen. This plugin collapses it to a narrow column of icons, so that the dashboards, the workflow and the settings have the room.

  • A click on an icon with a submenu (Dashboards, Settings, …) opens the whole menu as a drawer over the page, with that section expanded. The page doesn't move. The drawer closes again when a link is chosen, when the mouse leaves it, on a click outside or with Escape.
  • Icons with a direct link (e.g. Tools) just follow it.
  • Counts at the end of menu names, such as "My dossiers (2)" added by other plugins, are shown as badges on the icons and next to the names.
  • Each icon shows its name as a tooltip; entries without an icon show the first letter of their name. The icon of the current page's section is marked.
  • A button at the top of the menu keeps it open as before; the choice is remembered in the browser.
  • Keyboard: the drawer opens when the focus enters the menu and closes when it leaves. The names stay readable for screen readers.
  • No database tables, no settings. English and German.
